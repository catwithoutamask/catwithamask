About me

Hi, my name is Catwithamask ("Cat" for short),
I am a hobby artist & writer and love to create stories!

Most of my stories have fantasy aspacts to them. I mostly draw my characters but you can also finds lots of other arts here! I use both traditional artsupplies as well as digital art. But be warned, I also draw a lot of dark themes.

About this Website

This site is a fun little project for me to experiment and learn more about webdesign as well as to express myself in a way no social media can provide.

This also means it is a constant work in progress and will change over time. Some contents are currently not available as I haven't had time to work on them. But I am planning on expanding on them eventually.

Following subpage is still in planning:
Stories ➞ Here I want to write a bit about the stories that I write. Short descriptions about them and maybe share some bits and pieces of things I have written.

If you have any questions, you can contact me.
